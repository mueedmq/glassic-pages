Our story

A little more care,
at every step.

We’re Glassic: a Pakistan-based company bringing thoughtful products from closer to home.

Since 2018

It started with a better kind of container.

Glassic began with the idea that glass is a thoughtful material for packaging. We wanted to make better-quality options easier to find in a market where local glass bottle manufacturing remains limited.

In 2020, we began working with another resource found close to home: pink rock salt from the Salt Range of Pakistan. We source it carefully and take care through each step from the mines to you.

Our journey is still growing. What stays the same is our focus on quality, honest origins and the belief that how a product is made matters.
