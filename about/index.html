From requirement to supply

Technical capability, connected to global suppliers.

Glassic is an engineering-led plant and machinery business. Our founder brings an engineering background, and our team has technical experience to help understand applications, evaluate requirements and discuss equipment specifications.

We work with international suppliers to identify plant and machinery for the job. Our process considers duty and capacity, process compatibility, operating conditions, equipment condition, documentation and delivery needs, while keeping quality in focus throughout the sourcing and supply discussion.

We support major industrial sectors including power, cement, fertilizer, textile and sugar in Pakistan and international markets. Our aim is to match the requirement with suitable equipment and coordinate its delivery with clear, practical communication.
